# Global Supply Chain & Logistics Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate end-to-end supply chain operational efficiency, international customer fulfillment performance, shipping carrier costs, and historical order revenue trends.

## Key Analytical Insights
* **Top Revenue Markets:** Evaluates sales totals across leading destination countries, highlighting key revenue drivers (e.g., USA, Germany).
* **Delivery Performance:** Measures average shipping fulfillment lead time across international destinations (Finland, Canada, Norway, Denmark, Mexico).
* **Freight Cost Analysis:** Compares average freight charges across different shipping modes/carriers (`ship_via`).
* **Global Customer Base:** Analyzes customer count distribution across international destination markets.
* **Historical Revenue Trends:** Tracks total order value before discount over multi-year operational timelines.

## Tools & Technologies
* **Data Visualization:** Power BI Desktop (Bar & Line Charts, Geographical Breakdowns)
* **Data Processing:** Power Query (Data cleaning, date transformations & aggregation)
* **Reporting:** Operational dashboard structuring & PDF exporting
